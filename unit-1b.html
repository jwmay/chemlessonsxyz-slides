Temperature

Temperature measures the average kinetic energy of the particles in an object.
Kelvin (K)
0 K
SI unit for temperature.
Based on absolute zero — where particle motion stops.
Celsius (°C)
0° · 100°
Used in science.
Water freezes at 0°C, boils at 100°C.
Fahrenheit (°F)
32° · 212°
Not generally used in science.
Water freezes at 32°F, boils at 212°F.
K = °C + 273°C = K − 2731 K = 1 °C in size

UNCERTAINTY & SIG FIGS

Reading Instruments Correctly

Read the Meniscus
💧
The meniscus is the curved surface of a liquid. Always read volume from the bottom of the curve.
Avoid Parallax
👀
Parallax errors happen when you view from an angle. Read at eye level, straight on.
Use a Thermometer
🌡️
Don’t let the bulb touch the container — you’d measure the glass, not the solution.

UNCERTAINTY & SIG FIGS

Counting Significant Figures

Rule 1 · Non-zero digits
All non-zero numbers are always significant.
38.15 4 SF122.55 5 SF
Rule 2 · Leading zeros
Leading zeros are NOT significant.
0.008 1 SF0.0156 3 SF
Rule 3 · Captive zeros
Zeros between non-zeros are significant.
50.8 3 SF2001 4 SF
Rule 4 · Trailing zeros
Significant only with a decimal point.
200. 3 SF25,000 2 SF

Calculating with Sig Figs

Multiply / Divide
Answer keeps the fewest significant figures.
(13.91)(23.3) = 324.103
324 g (3 SF)
Add / Subtract
Answer keeps the fewest decimal places.
3.75 + 4.1 = 7.85

UNCERTAINTY & SIG FIGS

Percent Error

Percent error compares a measurement to its accepted value — it indicates accuracy.
% error =
|your value − actual|actual value× 100
WORKED EXAMPLE
A density is measured as 1.40 g/mL; the accepted value is 1.36 g/mL.
|1.40 − 1.36|1.36 × 100
% error = 2.9%

SCIENTIFIC NOTATION

Writing Scientific Notation

M × 10n
M — a number between 1 and 10
n — an integer (the exponent)
The Steps
Move the decimal until one digit sits to its left.
Places moved = exponent.
Keep only significant figures.
Sign of the Exponent
Decimal moves left positive
65,000 6.5 × 104
Decimal moves right negative
0.0000579 5.79 × 10−5
SCIENTIFIC NOTATION

Calculating in Scientific Notation

Add / Subtract
Exponents must match first — then add or subtract the M values; bring the exponent down.
(4 × 106) + (3 × 106)
= 7 × 106
Multiply / Divide
Multiply/divide M; add exponents to multiply, subtract to divide.
(2 × 105)(3 × 102)
= 6 × 107
(6 × 105) ÷ (3 × 102)
= 2 × 103
DIMENSIONAL ANALYSIS & GRAPHING
Dimensional Analysis
A systematic approach that uses conversion factors to convert from one unit to another.
A conversion factor is a ratio of equivalent values with different units.
CONVERSION FACTORS
1 min = 60 sec
1 ft = 12 in
1 m = 100 cm
1 mol = 6.02 × 1023 atoms
DIMENSIONAL ANALYSIS & GRAPHING

Graphing Data

A graph is a visual display of data that makes trends easier to see than a table.
Circle / Pie
Wedges represent percentages of a fixed whole.
Bar
Shows how a quantity varies across categories.
Line
Independent on the x-axis, dependent on the y-axis.
DIMENSIONAL ANALYSIS & GRAPHING

Graphing Relationships

Directly Proportional
As one variable increases, the other also increases. Its graph is a straight line.
Inversely Proportional
As one variable increases, the other decreases. Its graph is a hyperbola.
