RADIATION BASICS

Gamma Rays (γ)

Gamma rays are photons — high-energy electromagnetic radiation.
Because photons have no mass and no charge, gamma emission does not change the atomic number or mass number of the nucleus.
Gamma rays almost always accompany alpha and beta radiation — but since they don’t affect mass or atomic number, they’re usually omitted from nuclear equations.

NUCLEAR STABILITY & DECAY
Nuclear Stability
The protons and neutrons in a nucleus are together called nucleons.
Although protons are positively charged and repel each other, they stay close together because of the strong nuclear force.
The strong nuclear force overcomes the repulsion between protons to hold the nucleus together.

NUCLEAR STABILITY & DECAY

The Band of Stability

When you plot the number of neutrons versus the number of protons, all stable nuclei fall in an area called the band of stability.
☢️ All nuclei outside the band are radioactive and undergo decay in order to gain stability.

Transmutation

🔀
Except for gamma emission, radioactive decay converts one element into another — a reaction that changes an atom’s atomic number is called transmutation.
⚖️
Whether an atom decays — and what type of radiation it emits — depends on its neutron-to-proton ratio.

NUCLEAR EQUATIONS & HALF-LIFE

Radiochemical Dating

Determining the age of an object by measuring how much of a certain radioisotope remains in it.
🪨 Uranium-238
Commonly used to date objects such as rocks.
By measuring U-238 remaining in meteorites, scientists estimated the age of the solar system.
4.6 × 109 years
🦴 Carbon-14
Measures the age of objects that were once part of a living organism — bones, wood, cloth.
When a plant or animal dies, its C-14 begins to decay to nitrogen-14:
146C 0−1e+147N
Half-life 5,730 yr limited to objects under 50,000 years old.
🔺
C-14 dating of ash & charcoal puts the Great Pyramid of Giza at more than 4,000 years old.
FISSION, FUSION & USES

Induced Transmutation

Transmutation can be forced — bombarding a stable nucleus with a neutron or other high-energy particle is called induced transmutation.
The bombarding particle must move at extremely high speeds to overcome the electrostatic repulsion with the target nucleus.
Particle accelerators are machines that produce the high-speed particles needed to induce transmutation, using very strong electrostatic and magnetic fields.
Transuranium elements — those with atomic numbers greater than 92 — have all been produced by induced transmutation in labs, and all are radioactive.
FISSION, FUSION & USES

Nuclear Fission & Chain Reactions

The splitting of a heavy nucleus into two smaller, more stable nuclei is called nuclear fission. Each fission releases additional neutrons, which can cause more fissions — a self-sustaining chain reaction.
10n+23592U 14156Ba+9236Kr+3 10n
Subcritical mass
📉
Not massive enough to sustain a chain reaction — the reaction dies out.
Critical mass
⚖️
Massive enough to sustain a chain reaction — neutrons from one fission cause others.
Supercritical mass
💥
More than critical mass — the reaction can run out of control. Atomic bombs are supercritical fission.

☀️ Nuclear Fusion
The combining of two light nuclei to form a heavier, more stable nucleus.
Stars — including the sun — produce their energy this way, and large amounts of energy are released.
Requires extremely high temperatures, so these are called thermonuclear reactions.

FISSION, FUSION & USES

Detecting & Using Radiation

📻 Detecting
Radiation energetic enough to ionize matter is called ionizing radiation.
The ions formed produce a momentary electric current, which can be detected and counted.
A Geiger counter is a metal tube filled with a gas that the radiation ionizes.
🩺 Radiotracers
Radiotracers are radioactive nuclides introduced into organisms so their pathways can be traced by monitoring radioactivity.
24Na — blood flow
131I — thyroid activity
123I — brain imaging
133Xe — lung imaging
1 in every 3 hospital patients will undergo a nuclear medicine procedure.
⚕️ Radiation Therapy
Radiation can damage or destroy healthy cells — a serious health risk.
But it can also destroy unhealthy cells, such as cancer cells.
Despite destroying some healthy cells too, radiation therapy has become one of the most effective cancer treatments.
