STATES & PROPERTIES
What Is Matter?
Matter is anything that has mass and takes up space.
The physical forms of matter are called the states of matter.
🌍
MASS
+ SPACE
STATES & PROPERTIES

The Four States of Matter

Solid
Definite shape & volume.
Particles packed tight in fixed positions, vibrating in place.
Liquid
Definite volume, indefinite shape.
Particles close but not fixed.
Gas
Neither definite volume nor shape.
Particles spread far apart, in constant motion.
Plasma
Only at high temperatures.
Like a gas, but particles are ionized (charged).
STATES & PROPERTIES

Physical Properties

A physical property can be observed or measured without changing the sample’s composition — e.g. color, melting point.
Extensive
⚖️
Depends on the amount of matter present.
e.g. mass, volume
Intensive
🔍
Depends on the identity of the substance, not the amount.
e.g. density, boiling point, conductivity
STATES & PROPERTIES

Chemical Properties & Changes

A chemical property is the ability of a substance to combine with or change into other substances — e.g. flammability, rusting.
Physical Change
🧊
Alters a substance without changing its composition.
crushing, tearing, phase changes, dissolving
Chemical Change
🔥
One or more substances turn into new substances.
burning, cooking, rusting
STATES & PROPERTIES

Signs of a Chemical Change

Watch for these four clues that a new substance has formed:
🎨Change in color
💨Gas formed (fizzing) or odor
🧊Precipitate (solid) forms
🔥Light or heat produced

CLASSIFYING MATTER

Pure Substances vs. Mixtures

Pure Substance
One kind of material with a unique set of properties.
Elements and compounds are pure substances.
CuH2ONaCl
Mixture
A blend of two or more pure substances.
Cu + Sn bronze
H2O + NaCl salt water
CLASSIFYING MATTER

Two Kinds of Mixture

Homogeneous
🧂💧
Appears the SAME throughout.
e.g. salt water, air
Heterogeneous
🪨🥗
Has visibly DIFFERENT parts.
e.g. granite, oil & vinegar
CLASSIFYING MATTER

The Tyndall Effect

A laser is visible when particles are big enough to scatter light — this sorts mixtures into three types:
Solution
🧴
Very small particles
(−) Tyndall
Don’t settle
e.g. rubbing alcohol
Colloid
🥛
Medium particles
(+) Tyndall
Don’t settle
e.g. milk
Suspension
🍋
Large particles
(+) Tyndall
Particles settle
e.g. fresh lemonade
CLASSIFYING MATTER

Separating Mixtures

🧫
Filtration
Separates a solid from a liquid — filter paper catches the solid, liquid passes through.
♨️
Distillation
Uses differences in boiling point — the lower-boiling liquid evaporates first.
💎
Crystallization
The liquid evaporates, leaving solid crystals behind.
🌈
Chromatography
Separates pigments as they travel through special paper at different rates.
