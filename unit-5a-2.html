CLASSIFYING REACTIONS
Classifying Chemical Reactions
Chemists classify chemical reactions in order to organize the many reactions that occur.
Knowing the categories can help you remember and understand them — and recognize patterns to predict the products.
There are 5 basic types: synthesis, combustion, decomposition, and replacement (single and double).

TYPES OF REACTIONS

Synthesis (Combination) Reactions

A reaction in which two or more reactants come together to form a single product.
GENERAL FORM
A + B AB
*A and B can be elements or simple compounds.
EXAMPLES
4Fe + 3O2 2Fe2O3
2Na + Cl2 2NaCl
TYPES OF REACTIONS

Decomposition Reactions

A reaction in which a single compound is broken down into two or more simpler compounds or elements.
GENERAL FORM
AB A + B
EXAMPLES
2H2O 2H2 + O2
CaCO3 CaO + CO2

TYPES OF REACTIONS

Single Replacement Reactions

A reaction in which an uncombined element replaces an element that is part of a compound.
GENERAL FORM
A + BX AX + B
EXAMPLES
Mg + CuSO4 MgSO4 + Cu
3Fe + 4H2O Fe3O4 + 4H2
TYPES OF REACTIONS

Single Replacement: The Cut-In 🕺

A lone element “cuts in” on a couple — and steals a partner.
Mg+HClMgCl+H
Mg + 2HCl MgCl2 + H2
ACTIVITY SERIES
The Activity Series
Not all elements can replace all other elements in a replacement reaction.
The ability of an element to react is the element’s activity. The more readily it reacts, the greater its activity.
An activity series lists elements by the ease with which they undergo single replacement reactions.

TYPES OF REACTIONS

Double Replacement Reactions

A reaction in which the atoms or ions from two different compounds replace each other.
GENERAL FORM
AX + BY AY + BX
The two compounds trade partners — like swapping dance partners. 💃🕺
EXAMPLES
CaCO3 + 2HCl CaCl2 + H2CO3
TYPES OF REACTIONS

Combustion Reactions

A reaction in which a substance combines with oxygen, releasing a large amount of energy in the form of heat and light.
GENERAL FORM
CxHy + O2 CO2 + H2O
*Combustion of hydrocarbons always forms CO2 and water. 🔥
EXAMPLES
C3H8 + 5O2 3CO2 + 4H2O

SOLUTIONS

Solutions

Solution
🥤
Homogeneous mixtures of two or more substances.
Solvent
💧
The dissolving medium of a solution; present in the greater amount.
Solute
🧂
The substance dissolved in a solvent to form a solution.
Water dissolves a wide variety of substances. Solutions in which water is the solvent are called aqueous solutions — the (aq) symbol!
IONIC EQUATIONS

Ionic Equations

Until this point we have only used formula equations:
HCl(aq) + NaOH(aq) H2O(l) + NaCl(aq)
A formula equation shows the complete chemical formulas of the reactants and products.
It is often useful to show whether dissolved substances are present mostly as ions or as molecules.
IONIC EQUATIONS

Ionic Compounds in Water 💧

When an ionic compound dissolves, each ion is separated and dispersed throughout the solution — dissociation.
As it dissolves, the ions separate and become surrounded by H2O molecules.
O end faces Na+ · H ends face Cl−
IONIC EQUATIONS

Complete & Net Ionic Equations

FORMULA EQUATION
HCl(aq) + NaOH(aq) H2O(l) + NaCl(aq)
COMPLETE IONIC EQUATION — all aqueous ionic compounds written as ions
H+(aq) + Cl−(aq) + Na+(aq) + OH−(aq) H2O(l) + Na+(aq) + Cl−(aq)
NET IONIC EQUATION — spectator ions removed
H+(aq) + OH−(aq) H2O(l)
Spectator ions appear in identical forms on both sides — present, but they play no role in the reaction. (Here: Na+ and Cl−.)
IONIC EQUATIONS

Writing a Net Ionic Equation

The net ionic equation includes only the ions and molecules directly involved in the reaction.

SOLUTIONS

Types of Reactions in Aqueous Solution

When two aqueous solutions containing ions react, three types of products can form:
Precipitates
🌨️
An insoluble solid forms.
2NaOH(aq) + CuCl2(aq)
2NaCl(aq) + Cu(OH)2(s)
Water
💧
An acid and a base form water.
HBr(aq) + NaOH(aq)
H2O(l) + NaBr(aq)
Gases
🫧
A gas bubbles out of the solution.
2HI(aq) + Li2S(aq)
H2S(g) + 2LiI(aq)
