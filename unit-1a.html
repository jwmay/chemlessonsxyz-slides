CHEMISTRY & MATTER

Three Scales of Matter

Macroscopic
👁️
Big enough to observe with the naked eye — no microscope needed.
Seen with: your eyes
Microscopic
🔬
Too small to see unaided — a microscope is required.
Seen with: a microscope
Submicroscopic
⚛️
So tiny even optical microscopes can’t see them — like atoms.
Seen with: neither

Substances & Chemicals
A substance is matter that has a definite composition — also known as a chemical.
Ozone is an example of a substance.
Ozone (O3) forms when oxygen gas (O2) is exposed to ultraviolet radiation.
O3
OZONE

CHEMISTRY & MATTER

Mass vs. Weight

Mass
🧱
A measurement of the amount of matter.
constant everywhere
Weight
🌍
A measure of mass and the force of gravity on an object.
changes place to place
⚖️ Weight can change from place to place — but mass is always constant.

THE SCIENTIFIC METHOD

A systematic approach

A repeated cycle used in scientific study — run again and again until a hypothesis is supported or discarded.
1
Observe
gather information
2
Hypothesize
propose an answer
3
Experiment
test the hypothesis
4
Analyze
study the data
5
Conclude
support or discard
↻ Repeat until the hypothesis is supported or discarded
THE SCIENTIFIC METHOD

Making Observations

An observation is the act of gathering information.
Qualitative
🎨
Describes qualities — color, smell, shape, texture.
Comes from the five senses.
👁️ “the liquid is blue”
Quantitative
🔢
Describes amounts with numbers — how much, how big, how fast.
Always measured.
📏 “25 mL of liquid”
THE SCIENTIFIC METHOD

Hypothesis & Experiment

💡
A hypothesis is a tentative explanation for what has been observed.
🧪
An experiment is a set of controlled observations that test the hypothesis.
THE SCIENTIFIC METHOD

Variables

A variable is a quantity or condition that can have more than one value.
Independent
🔧
The variable you plan to change.
You control it
Dependent
📊
The variable that changes in response to the independent variable.
You measure it
THE SCIENTIFIC METHOD

Control & Conclusion

⚖️
A control is a standard for comparison in the experiment.
✅
A conclusion is a judgment based on the information obtained from the experiment.
THE SCIENTIFIC METHOD

Theory & Law

Theory
💡
An explanation repeatedly supported by many experiments.
States a broad principle of nature.
Succeeds when it makes predictions that come true.
Scientific Law
📜
A relationship in nature supported by many experiments.
No exceptions have ever been found.
SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH

Three Kinds of Research

Pure
🔍
Research to gain knowledge for its own sake.
Applied
🛠️
Research to solve a specific problem.
Chance
🎲
Results far different from what was expected.
