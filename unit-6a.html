Counting Atoms
Knowing the mass of 1 atom or molecule is important, but it is not practical to use such units in the lab.
The gram is the desired unit of mass.
Chemists needed a way to relate the atomic mass of an element to an amount of that element in grams.

THE MOLE

The Mole

The number of atoms of an element whose mass in grams equals its atomic mass is 6.02 × 1023. This is called the MOLE.
A COUNTING UNIT
1 mol = 6.02 × 1023
🥚 1 dozen eggs = 12 eggs
1 mole of eggs =
602,000,000,000,000,000,000,000 eggs
THE MOLE
Where Does “Mole” Come From?
The word “mole” was introduced by Wilhelm Ostwald, from the word moles, meaning a “heap” or “pile.”
We are just defining the pile as containing 6.02 × 1023 items.
Because it is such a huge number, we usually reserve the mole for atoms or molecules.
⛰️
1 MOLE IS ALWAYS
6.02 × 1023 items

MOLAR MASS
Molar Mass
While the number of items in a mole is always the same, the mass of that mole varies.
The mass in grams of 1 mole of a substance is its molar mass. It depends on the masses of the particles that make up the substance.
It is the same number as the atomic mass or formula mass — in grams per mole (g/mol).

SAME COUNT
different mass

MOLAR VOLUME OF A GAS
Molar Volume of a Gas
With matter in the gas phase, we can develop another equality.
Under the same conditions, equal volumes of gases contain equal numbers of particles.
1 mol of a gas occupies the same volume as 1 mol of any other gas under the same conditions.
🎈
EQUAL VOLUMES
equal numbers of particles

PERCENT COMPOSITION
Percent Composition
The mass of each element in a compound relative to the total mass of the compound.
Found by dividing the mass of the element by the mass of the compound and multiplying the quotient by 100%.
All the percents in a compound add up to 100%.
%
PART ÷ WHOLE
× 100%
PERCENT COMPOSITION

The Percent Composition Equation

Part ÷ whole × 100%.
% element =mass of elementmass of compound× 100%
Mass of element = atomic mass × number of atoms in the formula
Mass of compound = the formula (molar) mass
